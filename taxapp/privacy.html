Privacy & Policy

1. Information Collection

To provide our financial and tax services, CM Tax Experts collects personal data including your Name, PAN, Mobile Number, Email, Bank Statements, and Income Statements.

2. Data Usage

Your information is used strictly for the purpose of computing tax liabilities, filing Income Tax Returns (ITR), establishing business entities, and ensuring regulatory compliance on your behalf.

3. Payment Security

We integrate with Razorpay and official UPI channels (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). We do not store your credit card information directly on our servers. All transactions are routed through secure, encrypted gateways.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We will not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information. We may share necessary details solely with government portals (e.g., the Income Tax Department of India) to fulfill your requested services.

5. Contact Us

For concerns regarding your data privacy, please contact our support desk directly via the app dashboard or email privacy@cmexperts.com.
